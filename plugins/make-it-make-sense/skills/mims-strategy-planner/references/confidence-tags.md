# Confidence tags

Tag every claim in the strategy doc and evidence appendix with one of these.

| Tag | Meaning | Can you decide on it? |
|---|---|---|
| `[VERIFIED]` | Confirmed by a source you can cite (customer data, signed deal, external research, documented behavior) | Yes |
| `[HYPOTHESIS]` | A working belief with reasoning or partial support, not yet confirmed | Yes, if confidence is high — and it goes on the "to validate" list |
| `[SINGLE POINT]` | Rests on one anecdote, one customer, or one conversation | Not alone — investigate before building on it |
| `[PROBE]` | An idea worth testing in live conversation; not a claim you stand behind | No — never in published copy |

Rules:
- Put a source next to every `[VERIFIED]` item in the evidence appendix.
- Every `[HYPOTHESIS]` that a locked stage depends on appears on the "to validate"
  list, tied to an objective's validation instrument.
- If you don't actually hold a claim, delete it — don't keep it with a tag.
- A `[SINGLE POINT]` result can prompt an investigation but can't unlock a stage
  by itself.
