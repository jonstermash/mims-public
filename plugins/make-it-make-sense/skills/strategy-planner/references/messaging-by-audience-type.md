# Messaging by audience type

Read during Stage 4. The primary is always an outcome; *whose* outcome depends on
the audience type. Secondary messages stay at 200 characters max in every case.

## Buyers
Primary = the outcome *they* get. Secondary = the proof or features behind it.

## Channel / resellers (they sell on your behalf)
- Primary = the **customer outcome** — that's what they'll be selling, and what a
  good partner actually wants (happy customers, kept relationships).
- Their *own* upside (revenue, retention) is a **consequence of customer value**.
  Put it in a secondary, framed through customer success. Never lead with it.
- Avoid "you'll make money / win bigger deals" — it assumes a mercenary partner
  and reads badly. A bigger deal means nothing if it doesn't deliver customer value.

## Influence / pull audiences (they don't buy)
Example: a brand whose *suppliers* are the real customers.
- Message indirectly: "when your suppliers use this, you get…"
- Usually vision-forward.
- Each secondary points to the product feature that backs the claim. Roadmap
  status is flagged in the strategy doc only (see the build-status rule in SKILL.md).

## Missing-information trap audiences (any type)
If Stage 3 flagged the audience as not knowing what they're missing, lead with the
felt pain they'd recognize, then the outcome. A feature list alone won't land.
