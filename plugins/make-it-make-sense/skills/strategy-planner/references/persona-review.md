# Adversarial persona review

Before locking any asset, stress-test it through the eyes of each Stage 3 audience
hearing about you for the first time. Asset QA catches what's wrong on the page;
this catches what's wrong in the reader's head.

## How to run it
- **One pass per core audience.** Become that person — role, self-interest,
  behavior — landing on the asset **cold, with ~5 seconds of patience**.
- **React in their voice, honestly.** Where do they squint, where do they bounce?
  Quote the exact line that loses them.
- **Run each persona independently.** Reading them all in one pass smooths over
  conflicts; separate takes surface contradictions *between* audiences.

## The four cold-reader tests
1. **"What is this?"** — can they tell what you do / what they'd get in five
   seconds? (The internal Goal is usually too abstract to be the first thing read.)
2. **"Is it for me?"** — can they self-identify fast?
3. **"Do you get my problem?"** — does it reveal a pain they'd recognize, or list
   features they must translate? Check hardest for audiences flagged as
   missing-information traps in Stage 3.
4. **"Is this real?"** — does the skeptic (gatekeeper, finance, technical persona)
   hit a credibility or vaporware reflex? Where's the proof anchor?

## Watch for the tuning trap
An asset can be beautifully tuned to the audience that *doesn't* buy (a homepage
that speaks to the influence audience while the converting buyer bounces).

## Output
- A short cold reaction per persona, in their voice.
- Cross-cutting problems, ranked most-damaging first.
- The 2–3 highest-leverage fixes. Fixes should *strengthen* the strategy, not
  retreat from it — usually the asset led with the destination and skipped the doorway.
- If the same problem shows up across assets, it's likely a Stage 3 or 4 issue —
  see "Reopening a locked stage" in SKILL.md.
